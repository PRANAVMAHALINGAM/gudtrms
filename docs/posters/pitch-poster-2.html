The mediator's math
Knaster's procedure
1You each privately value every outcome in dollars.
2Each item goes where it's worth the most in total.
3Fair share = half your own total.
4A buyout leaves you both equally above it.
Buyout = (EA − EB) ÷ 2
The advocate
An LLM with a veto
Claude judges each deal. Code overrules a yes unless:
the total is under their cap
every dealbreaker is met
the move-out date is in their window
they get at least their fair share
If the LLM fails, the code decides.
